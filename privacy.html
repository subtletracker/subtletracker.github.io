Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

Subtle is designed as a private, local-first subscription tracker. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is handled.

Last updated November 2026

No account, no advertising SDKs, no analytics tracking, and no Subtle-operated servers.

Overview

Subtle helps you track subscriptions, recurring bills, renewal dates, and related spending. The app is built to keep your subscription data local to your device.

Data Collection

Subtle does not collect, sell, or store personal data on servers operated by the developer. The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, or third-party data processors.

Exchange Rates

To convert amounts between currencies, Subtle downloads the public daily reference rates of the European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu). This request contains no information about you or your subscriptions. Like any website, the ECB server receives your IP address as part of the connection.

Data Stored On Your Device

Subtle stores the information you choose to enter so the app can provide its core features:

  • Subscription names, prices, billing cycles, billing dates, categories, payment methods, and cancellation dates.
  • Payment history generated from your saved subscriptions.
  • Custom templates you create.
  • Your display name and avatar, if you choose to add them.
  • App preferences such as currency, budget, onboarding state, and App Lock setting.

Notifications

If you enable renewal reminders, Subtle schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content is generated locally and is not sent to Subtle servers.

App Lock

If you enable App Lock, authentication is handled by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Subtle does not receive or store your Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode data.

Backups

Subtle keeps automatic backup copies of your data in the app's own storage on your device (the latest five). Your device settings may include this data in encrypted device backups or iCloud backups managed by Apple. Subtle does not operate its own backup service.

iCloud Sync

Subtle Pro offers optional iCloud sync, which is off by default. When you turn it on, your subscription data is stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so it stays in sync across your devices. This data is handled by Apple under your iCloud account and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Subtle Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details; the app only checks with Apple whether Subtle Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account.

Data Deletion

You can delete subscriptions and templates inside the app. You can also delete all app data by deleting Subtle from your device.

Children's Privacy

Subtle is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support through the Support page.
